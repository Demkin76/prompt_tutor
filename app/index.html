The thesis

Natural language becomes game logic.

GOLEM turns prompt engineering into a visible system. A charter is not a hint. It is the policy an autonomous unit follows while the world changes underneath it.

01 / WRITE⌁

Define behavior, not clicks.

Write priorities, constraints and recovery rules. Direct movement controls do not exist.

02 / LOCK◇

The world arrives after.

The map is revealed only after submission. Hard-coded routes break. Policies have to generalize.

03 / PROVE✓

Every win has evidence.

Deterministic world state, event traces and tests show exactly why a run passed or failed.

Progression that changes the question

Every level teaches a new kind of agency.

Red Floor asks the player to express a constraint. Maze adds exploration under fog of war. Tower Defense flips the problem: read the opponent's rules, then write a counter-policy.

01
